## How I Approach Analytics

1. **Understand the business question:** Clarify the decision or problem the analysis needs to support.
2. **Prepare and validate the data:** Review structure, completeness, consistency, and potential data quality issues.
3. **Analyze:** Use appropriate spreadsheet, SQL, or Python techniques to investigate metrics, trends, and exceptions.
4. **Communicate:** Present findings through reports, dashboards, and concise business recommendations.
